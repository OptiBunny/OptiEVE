Public EVE Online manufacturing catalog

The accompanying JSON is a reduced, versioned projection of CCP Games' official
EVE Online Static Data Export. It contains published inventory names/categories
and published blueprint manufacturing materials, products, skill requirements
and base activity times. It contains no account data or private preferences.

Source and automation documentation:
https://developers.eveonline.com/docs/services/static-data/
https://developers.eveonline.com/static-data/

The exact build, release date, official archive URL, archive SHA256 and selected
source-file SHA256 values are recorded inside the JSON. Refresh using the
repository's update-manufacturing-catalog script, review the result, and rebuild.
Runtime calls may check the official latest-build manifest; they never replace
the pinned catalog or download the complete SDE automatically.
